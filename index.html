ABOOL — Agricultural Traceability & Compliance Platform

ABOL is an open-source digital infrastructure platform for agricultural supply-chain traceability, geospatial verification, compliance, and product provenance.

ABOL is designed to connect farmers, cooperatives, unions, processors, exporters, regulators, certification bodies, and international buyers through a shared digital traceability infrastructure.

The platform initially focuses on Ethiopian coffee, with a strong emphasis on EUDR compliance, farm-level geolocation, deforestation-risk verification, and digital product passports.

🌍 Vision

Build trusted digital infrastructure that makes agricultural products traceable, verifiable, compliant, and globally accessible.

ABOL aims to transform fragmented agricultural supply-chain data into a connected digital system where every product can be traced back to its origin.

🎯 What ABOL Solves

Agricultural supply chains often suffer from:

  • Fragmented producer information
  • Limited farm-level traceability
  • Manual record keeping
  • Poor geospatial data
  • Difficult EUDR compliance
  • Lack of standardized digital records
  • Limited visibility between supply-chain actors
  • Difficulty verifying product origin
  • Expensive compliance processes
  • Weak interoperability between existing systems

ABOL provides a digital infrastructure layer to address these challenges.

2. GeoVerify

ABOL GeoVerify is the geospatial verification component of ABOL.

It is designed to verify agricultural plots against geospatial and environmental datasets.

Key capabilities include:

  • GPS point capture
  • Polygon mapping
  • Plot-area calculation
  • GeoJSON support
  • Satellite-data integration
  • Historical land-cover analysis
  • Deforestation-risk assessment
  • Coordinate validation
  • Spatial intersection analysis
  • Evidence generation

🌳 EUDR Compliance

ABOL is being designed to support supply-chain actors preparing evidence for the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR).

The platform can organize information required for due-diligence workflows, including:

  • Country of production
  • Farm/plot geolocation
  • Product information
  • Commodity information
  • Producer/supplier information
  • Production volume
  • Traceability relationships
  • Legal-compliance evidence
  • Geospatial verification
  • Deforestation-risk assessment
  • Due-diligence documentation

ABOL is intended to support compliance workflows and evidence management. It does not replace the legal responsibility of operators or competent authorities.

📱 ABOL Mobile

ABOL includes an offline-first mobile data-capture architecture for field operations.

Field workers can:

  1. Register farmers
  2. Register farms
  3. Register plots
  4. Capture GPS coordinates
  5. Map plot boundaries
  6. Calculate plot areas
  7. Record agricultural information
  8. Store records offline
  9. Synchronize data when connectivity returns
Mobile App
    ↓
Local SQLite Database
    ↓
Offline Sync Queue
    ↓
ABOL API
    ↓
Central Database

This architecture is designed for rural environments where reliable internet connectivity may not always be available.

🔐 Data Integrity

ABOL is designed around the principle that traceability data should be:

  • Verifiable
  • Auditable
  • Timestamped
  • Consistent
  • Permission-controlled
  • Interoperable

Where appropriate, cryptographic technologies may be used to provide additional integrity and provenance guarantees.

Blockchain is not required for the core ABOL architecture.
